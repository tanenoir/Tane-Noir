Tane Noir, is a 16-year-old Indian-American independent artist whose musical identity is built around darkness, faith, love, and heartbreak.

Tane Noir's music focuses on emotions that are difficult to explain in ordinary words. His songs explore love, longing, memories, heartbreak, and the darker emotions that can exist alongside something beautiful. Rather than following a single formula, he uses music as a way to tell stories and turn personal feelings into something listeners can experience for themselves.

His sound is shaped by light, deep, and moody vocals, combined with simple and relatable songwriting and a dark, mysterious atmosphere. His vocal delivery is one of the most important parts of his identity as an artist, allowing the emotion behind his lyrics to remain at the center of every song.

Among his major inspirations are Chris Grey and David Kushner, particularly their ability to create emotional, atmospheric music with a strong sense of identity. Tane Noir takes inspiration from that world while developing his own sound and storytelling style.

His Music

At the heart of Tane Noir's music is love.

His songs explore love from different perspectives — the feeling of falling for someone, the beauty of being connected to another person, the fear of losing them, the memories that remain afterward, and the heartbreak that can follow.

His music is designed to make listeners feel two things strongly: lost inside a dark atmosphere and connected to the feeling of heartbreak.

Storytelling is the foundation of his creative approach. Every song is intended to feel like a chapter, a memory, or a moment from a larger story. His lyrics remain simple enough to feel relatable while maintaining a darker and more mysterious edge.

For Tane Noir, a song does not need to explain everything. Sometimes leaving something unexplained allows the listener to find their own story inside it.

He believes that every heartbreak leaves a story behind, that love can be both beautiful and painful, that music can express feelings people cannot say aloud, and that there can still be beauty within darkness.

The Beginning

Tane Noir began making music in 2023.

What started as an interest gradually became a serious creative journey. Instead of waiting for professional equipment or a traditional studio setup, he began learning and creating with the resources available to him.

He writes his own songs, performs his vocals, plays guitar, produces, records, and mixes his music himself — entirely on a mobile device.

Every part of the process is an opportunity to learn. From writing the first lyric to shaping the final sound, Tane Noir remains directly involved in his music.

His independence is an important part of his journey. The goal is not simply to have music released, but to understand how every piece of that music is created.

The Name Tane Noir

The name Tane Noir represents the darker side of his artistic identity.

The word “Noir”, associated with black and darkness, reflects the themes and atmosphere that surround his music. Darkness is not simply an aesthetic for Tane Noir — it represents the emotional space where many of his stories exist.

Love can have darkness.

Memories can have darkness.

Heartbreak can have darkness.

And sometimes, even within that darkness, something beautiful can remain.

Sarah Noir

One of the most personal elements of Tane Noir's story is Sarah Noir.

Sarah Noir is a name created for a real person; it is not her actual name. She holds a deeply personal place in Tane Noir's life and artistic world.

She is someone he fell deeply in love with — his love, his muse, a major source of inspiration, and someone connected to both the happiest and most painful emotions behind his stories.

Sarah Noir represents more than a single person within his artistic universe. She represents love, longing, memory, heartbreak, hope, and the desire to find someone who still matters.

Tane Noir refers to her as “Beautifully Wrong.”

The phrase describes a love that can feel incredibly beautiful while also carrying pain, confusion, distance, and heartbreak. It became closely connected to the planned Beautifully Wrong EP and the story surrounding it.

At the center of that story is a simple feeling:

Just someone trying to find her.

Personal Story

The Future

Tane Noir's vision extends beyond individual singles.

He wants to create connected EPs and albums that form a larger universe, where different songs and projects can exist as chapters of a continuing story.

His long-term goal is to build music into a full-time career, while continuing to develop his sound, songwriting, visuals, and artistic identity.

The journey is still young, but the direction is clear: create music that tells a story, build a world around it, and allow listeners to feel something real.
